SnapyCall Pilot Outreach

SnapyCall Pilot Outreach is an invitation-only research and recruiting workspace for finding and evaluating prospective experts who may be interested in the SnapyCall pilot.

What the app does

Authorized users can research publicly available candidate sources, record contact provenance, review expertise and suitability, and prepare draft outreach messages. Google sign-in verifies the invited user's identity. Separately, users may grant read-only access to their Google Forms response data and Google Sheets; granting access does not send messages or publish Google documents.

At this pilot stage, automatic email outreach and Google data synchronization are not enabled. Generated drafts require review. Users are responsible for appropriate permissions and lawful contact practices.
